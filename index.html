About the Firm

Reznik Eylon Architects
Jerusalem — Tel Aviv

Reznik Eylon Architects unites two long-established practices: David Reznik–Baruch Reznik Architects, founded in Jerusalem in 1958 by architect David Reznik — an Israel Prize laureate for architecture — and now led by his son, architect Baruch Reznik; and Anat Eylon Architects | archiTECHteam, founded in Tel Aviv in 2005 by architect Anat Eylon. Together, the joint office brings decades of experience and a shared language of restraint and precision to projects across Jerusalem, Tel Aviv, and beyond.

The practice specializes in high-rise towers and complex, mixed-use buildings alongside public and academic institutions, with deep expertise guiding projects through the statutory planning process. The firm currently leads a number of major urban-renewal projects and collaborates with architect Ian Bader of Pei Cobb Freed & Partners, New York.

Anat Eylon Architects | archiTECHteam

Founded in 2005 by architect Anat Eylon, archiTECHteam brings a distinct philosophy of imagination and minimalism to planning — advanced, sharp, simple, and precise design that responds honestly to place.

The office brings deep experience in multi-disciplinary parametric planning for complex projects, and adds unique value through project development, optimal planning solutions, and up-to-date technologies in advanced design and construction methods.

David Reznik–Baruch Reznik Architects

Founded in Jerusalem in 1958 by architect David Reznik — recipient of the Israel Prize and one of the great Israeli architects of his generation — and active in Jerusalem to this day, now led by architect Baruch Reznik for over 35 years.

The firm's project portfolio spans urban planning, public buildings, residential complexes, commercial projects, and interior design for a wide range of clients.
